## DH 3.3.3 facts
- `ClientApi.renderLods()` / `renderDeferredLodsForShaders()` -> `renderLodLayer`; fade passes `renderFadeOpaque/Transparent`.
- DH draws LODs from inside `LevelRenderer.renderChunkLayer`, so also during Oculus's shadow pass (the old light leak).
- `getTargetFramebuffer()` lowercase-b in 3.x. Forge `OculusAccessor` patched by `MixinDHOculusAccessor`.
- DH fade and TAA are off under shader packs by design. Old DH 2.x data shows as magenta/black checkers until regenerated.
- DH material IDs `DH_BLOCK_*` 0-15 match `EDhApiBlockMaterial`; any block with light emission > 0 becomes ILLUMINATED. Workaround for
  glowing modded plants: DH `ignoredRenderBlockCsv` (exact IDs).
